ACTIVITY 01 — HARDWARE PROTOTYPING

LED Interfacing with Breadboard

External LEDs were interfaced onto a solderless breadboard using an Arduino UNO microcontroller and programmed via the Arduino IDE. Current-limiting resistors were integrated in series to safeguard circuit components.

Digital output pins were configured to supply controlled voltage signals, establishing foundational understanding of breadboard continuity, polarity alignment, and hardware-software execution.

ACTIVITIES 02 & 03 — DIGITAL LOGIC & WIRELESS CONTROL

Binary Logic, Bluetooth LED Control and App Development

The onboard Arduino UNO LED (pin 13) was programmed to execute blinking patterns, verifying binary states: binary 1 (HIGH) turns the LED ON, while binary 0 (LOW) turns it OFF.

Wireless control was achieved by interfacing the HC-05 Bluetooth module via UART pins (TX/RX) with a custom mobile application to transmit serial commands for remote LED switching.
